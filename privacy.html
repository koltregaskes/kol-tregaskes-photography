Your information

Privacy notice.

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who runs this site and how to get in touch

Kol Tregaskes runs Kol Tregaskes Photography from the United Kingdom and is responsible for personal information he receives through this site. For privacy questions or requests, use the existing Contact page. This notice does not introduce a new email address or claim that a proposed contact address has been verified.

Hosting and request logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a page sends GitHub technical request information, including your IP address, browser user agent, requested URL and request time. GitHub controls its hosting logs; see the GitHub privacy statement. This notice does not promise a log retention period or claim that Kol receives those logs. A Cloudflare proxy is a proposal awaiting Kol's decision, not an active service in this site's code.

Google Fonts

All ten existing pages, and this privacy page, request Google Fonts. Your browser connects to fonts.googleapis.com for the stylesheet and fonts.gstatic.com for font files. These requests disclose your IP address and browser information, including the user agent, to Google. They are not self-hosted fonts. Read Google's own Google Fonts privacy statement for its explanation of font serving and data use.

Contact drafts and email

The contact helper reads the name, reply address or handle, subject and message you enter, checks them locally, and prepares an encoded mailto link. Opening the draft passes those fields to your own mail app. The site's code does not submit them to a form server or send an email. Nothing is sent by the draft helper until you choose to send from your mail app; your app and email provider then handle delivery. The direct email link also opens your own mail app.

If you send an enquiry, Kol receives the information you include and uses it to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information. This notice does not claim a verified delivery route or a fixed email retention period.

Browser storage and analytics

The current site JavaScript does not use localStorage or sessionStorage and does not set cookies. Your browser may cache site files and fonts and may offer its own form autofill. The site's code contains no active analytics script or collector. Cookieless analytics is planned under the existing work items #1498 and #367, but is not active. This notice must be reviewed before analytics or other data collection is enabled. Hosting logs and font requests still occur without analytics.

Shops, social sites and other links

Redbubble and 500px are outbound click-through links, not embedded shops or payment services. Social, related-site and news links also take you to other sites. Those destinations have their own privacy policies and may receive request and referrer information when you follow a link. This site does not establish whether those destinations use tracking, and makes no promise either way. The presence of a link does not itself mean the destination is loaded automatically.

Your UK data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK GDPR rights include being informed, access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and protections concerning automated decisions. Some rights depend on the reason for processing and are subject to exceptions. Use the Contact page to raise a request with Kol. For data controlled by a hosting, font or email provider, you can also contact that provider.

You can seek guidance or complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office: ICO complaints route. You can raise your concern with Kol first, but this does not remove your right to complain to the ICO.
